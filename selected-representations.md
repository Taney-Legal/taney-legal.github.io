## Litigation

- On behalf of a major healthcare insurer, prosecuted claims for breach of contract arising from a failed software development project and obtained an eight figure settlement from an international consulting firm responsible for the project
- Represented a client at trial in the bankruptcy of a wireless telecommunications company and secured for the client the right to control a number of wireless telecommunications licenses worth over $9 million
- On behalf of an American PVC door manufacturer, prosecuted claims of trademark and copyright infringement to a judgment of over $4 million and a permanent injunction against an infringing Chinese manufacturer
- Represented a pharmaceutical company in litigation over alleged theft of trade secrets, obtaining a multimillion dollar settlement payment and an agreement from the competitor to delay launch of its product
- Prosecuted claims of fraud, breach of contract, negligence and malpractice from a failed custom software development project relating to third party logistics enabling software and obtained a seven figure settlement from the international database technology company responsible for the project
- Defended a copyright infringement claim by an architect against a national residential homebuilder, obtaining a summary judgment dismissing all claims against the homebuilder
- Defended a manufacturer of a pharmaceutical “clean in place” system manufacturer in wrongful death lawsuit arising from workplace fatality in pharmaceutical plant and obtained summary judgment dismissing all claims against the manufacturer
- Key member of a litigation team that successfully represented, in multidistrict putative class actions, an international chemical company accused of fixing prices in the markets for hydrogen peroxide, plastics additives, polymethyl-methacrylate, methyl-methacrylate and monochloracetic acid, and obtained the dismissal of these actions
- Represented collective of mushroom growers in multidistrict putative class action arising from the collective’s alleged price fixing and monopolization, and was also key part of trial team that obtained defense verdict for the client against a regional supermarket chain asserting an “opt-out” claim alleging price fixing and monopolization on the part of the collective.
