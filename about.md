As Christians and Jews, we work to build relationships toward a just peace in
Israel/Palestine.

- We reach out to all faith-based communities to educate and engage.
- We challenge and encourage each other to explore the range of possible actions
  to foster justice and peace for all people of historic Palestine.
- We ground this work in our common religious tradition that inspires ethical
  living, respects human dignity, and guides us to speak out against the
  injustices we see in the world.
